====================
     2026-11-23
====================

   * Flashlight
- Torch strength no longer drops to almost nothing past the middle of the
  slider: the LED driver wrapped around above its limit.
- Seven working steps, from 50 to 190 mA. The old maximum was about 156 mA.
- Apps that only switch the torch on get the full level too.

   * Quick settings
- ColorOS panel: the tiles no longer disappear while it opens, and it opens
  faster.
- ColorOS panel in landscape: the carrier name keeps its place next to the
  status icons.
- New Sleep mode tile.

   * Lock screen
- The depth wallpaper subject stays off the media card.
- The Pulse visualizer shows again with the depth wallpaper on.
- The water unlock effects are skipped while the fluid clock is on.
- Descartes Laboratory says what the fluid clock needs to fly.

   * Dynamic island
- New Island style setting: Stock, PenguinOS or Lunaris. PenguinOS and Lunaris
  are the full islands of those ROMs, with their own music, events and cards.
- The Lunaris island has its own settings pages.
- A running timer no longer shows as an alarm.
- The setting shows up in the Settings search.

   * DeX
- The PenguinOS DeX app for Windows always gets a desktop. It used to show only
  the wallpaper and the navigation keys unless Use DeX was on.
- The dock shows on the desktop when the taskbar is off on the phone.

   * Freeform
- Opening a window from recents and from the game panel works again.

   * Battery and display
- Sleep mode (Settings > Battery): turns Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, mobile data and
  sensors off by hand or on a schedule, and restores them after a reboot.
- Idle manager (Settings > Battery): limits or closes the apps you choose while
  the screen is off. The list starts empty and system apps are left alone.
- An idle 120 Hz screen can drop to 60 Hz after 1 second instead of 3.

   * Google apps
- Removed: Feedback, Telemetry, Carrier Metrics, Partner Setup, Kids
  Supervision stub, Glasses stub, Print Recommendation Service and the two
  hotword enrollment apps. Extreme Battery Saver stays.

   * Changelog
- This page lists only the changes of this build.

   * Apps
- Battery Stats Viewer and Family Space are no longer included.
